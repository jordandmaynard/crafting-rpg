A NEW WILD EVERY TIME · 1024 × 1024 TILES · MEADOW · WOOD · ELDERWOOD · BOG · GRAVES · FROST · DESERT
  • You are a human adventurer, alone in an untamed land. Fell trees and break rocks for wood and stone, and raise a shelter before night falls.
  • WASD move, aim with the mouse, LMB attack (hold to keep swinging). Hit trees and rocks to gather.
  • Q whirlwind, E lunge, SPACE dash, F open and close doors.
  • B build mode: 1 floor, 2 wall, 3 door. LMB places, RMB removes for a full refund.
  • LS move, RS aim, RT attack (hold to keep swinging). Hit trees and rocks to gather.
  • X whirlwind, Y lunge, A dash, B open and close doors.
  • MENU build mode: X floors, Y walls, A doors (press again for the next kind), RB hearth. RS moves the build cursor, RT places (hold and move to lay a row), LT removes, B done.
  • Close a room with walls and doors and a roof goes up (rooms that meet across a wall share one roof). It hides while you are inside, and you heal faster under it.
  • Towns are safe. The wilds grow bolder at night.
  • M map (wheel zooms it and the minimap), P plot outlines on the maps, wheel to zoom, hold the right mouse button and drag (or Z / C) to turn the camera.
  • VIEW map, L3 plot outlines, DU DD zoom, DL DR turn the camera. An Xbox controller works as soon as you press a button on it.
