EMBERWILD

A NEW WILD EVERY TIME · 1024 × 1024 TILES · MEADOW · WOOD · ELDERWOOD · BOG · GRAVES · FROST · DESERT
  • You are a human adventurer, alone in an untamed land. Fell trees and break rocks for wood and stone, and raise a shelter before night falls.
  • WASD move, aim with the mouse, LMB attack (hold to keep swinging). Hit trees and rocks to gather.
  • Q whirlwind, E lunge, SPACE dash (a tumble you can steer), F open and close doors.
  • TAB inventory (your gear and pack, with your logs and stone): click to put on or take off, drag to move. Drag the window by its top to move it. Your weapon gives your attack and your Q and E; bare-handed you punch.
  • Wolves and boars leave a hide where they fall (FA picks it up; hides stack to 100). Logs and stone go straight into your pack, 200 to a square; when it's full they fall on the ground in piles of up to 200 to pick up. Every town has a tannery: use its tanning table to make leather armor, pants, boots and a helmet.
  • B build mode: 1 floor, 2 wall, 3 door. LMB places, RMB removes for a full refund.
  • LS move, RS aim, RT attack (hold to keep swinging). Hit trees and rocks to gather.
  • X whirlwind, Y lunge, B dash, A open and close doors, or jump down off a cliff edge.
  • The stick at the bottom left walks. The big button attacks: hold it to keep swinging, and drag it to aim. Drag an ability (whirlwind, lunge, dash) to aim it and let go to use it, or just tap it. Tap the prompts over doors, loot and the tanning table, swipe left or right on the view to turn the camera, pinch to zoom, and use the bar at the bottom for your pack, build mode and the map.
  • RB gear and pack: the D-pad picks a square, A puts on or takes off, B closes. Your weapon gives your attack and your X and Y; bare-handed you punch.
  • MENU build mode: X floors, Y walls, A doors (press again for the next kind), RB hearth. RS moves the build cursor, RT places (hold and move to lay a row), LT removes, B done.
  • Close a room with walls and doors and a roof goes up (rooms that meet across a wall share one roof). It hides while you are inside, and you heal faster under it.
  • Towns are safe. The wilds grow bolder at night.
  • M map (wheel zooms it and the minimap), P plot outlines on the maps, wheel to zoom, hold the right mouse button and drag (or Z / C) to turn the camera.
  • VIEW map, L3 plot outlines, DU DD zoom, DL DR turn the camera 45°, hold LT and move RS to turn it freely. An Xbox controller works as soon as you press a button on it.
