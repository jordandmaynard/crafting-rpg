Every sound here is synthesized as it loads: a strike through a material, grains for loose ground, rooms made from generated echoes, played a little differently every time. Pick a sound to tune it on the right. Your tuning is kept in this browser and the game uses it too; Copy settings gives the values to paste back so they become the defaults.

Sound starts on your first click (the browser's rule).
